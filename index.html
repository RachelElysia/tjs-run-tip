The maker

A project by Rachel Perkins

I built TJ's Run as my capstone at Hackbright Academy in 2021. I wanted to fix the annoying parts of cooking from a cookbook, so I took Trader Joe's recipe data and built the smoothest way I could think of to go from recipe to shopping list.

In 2026 I brought it back as a phone-first app with an AI pair programmer. The original 2021 version stays untouched as an artifact of my first solo fullstack app.

Fun fact: My entire dev career is viewable on GitHub.

  1. Q1 2021 · Hackbright Academy studentA web app. Flask, Postgres and Next.js, made for a laptop browser. Grocery lists by text with Twilio. The original
  2. Q4 2026 · v2, AI-integrated developmentA mobile app. Built phone-first: add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with a tab bar, keeps your favorites on your phone with no login, and works offline in the store. Under the hood: SQLite, Next 16 and React 19.
